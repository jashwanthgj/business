About Us

A legacy of devotion and service to humanity, blooming in the heart of Tiruttani.

Our History & Founder

Established in 1999 by K Subramaniya Swami

The seeds of Siva Sadhanam Trust were sown in the year 1999 in the peaceful surroundings of Jangalapalli Village, Tiruttani. It was founded by the visionary K Subramaniya Swami, a soul deeply moved by the teachings of Lord Shiva and the suffering of the underprivileged.

Witnessing the struggles of the homeless, the hunger of the destitute, and the crushed dreams of children who could not afford education, K Subramaniya Swami established this trust with a singular, unwavering focus: to serve humanity as a form of divine worship.

What began as a humble initiative to offer a few meals has now blossomed into a registered trust that actively manages an ashram, funds the education of countless youths, and feeds hundreds through weekly Annadanam. The light lit by Swami in 1999 continues to guide our every action today.

Our Goal & Mission

What is the goal of Siva Sadhanam Trust? Our goal is strictly rooted in humanitarian service, focusing on three core areas:

  • Helping poor students: We pay school and college fees so financial hardship does not stop a child's education.
  • Feeding the starving: Conducting Annadanam weekly and on all major Hindu festivals.
  • Sheltering the homeless: Providing a safe and caring ashram for those with nowhere else to go.
