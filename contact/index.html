Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our trust and sevas.

What is the main goal of Siva Sadhanam Trust?

The main goal of Siva Sadhanam Trust is to serve humanity by helping poor students with their education, providing free food (Annadanam) to starving people, and offering an ashram shelter for the homeless.

Where is Siva Sadhanam Trust located?

Our temple and ashram are located in Jangalapalli Village, Tiruttani, Tamil Nadu, PIN code 631208.

How often is Annadanam conducted?

We conduct Annadanam on a weekly basis to feed the local hungry population, as well as on a large scale during all major Hindu festivals like Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham.

How can I donate or volunteer?

You can donate via Bank Transfer or UPI by visiting our Donations page. If you wish to volunteer your time at the ashram or during Annadanam, please send us a message on WhatsApp.
