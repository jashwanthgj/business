Empowering the Future

Education Seva

Poverty should never be a barrier to a child's right to learn. Siva Sadhanam Trust actively identifies brilliant, hardworking students from impoverished backgrounds and takes full responsibility for their educational expenses.

We pay school and college fees directly to the institutions and provide essential supplies like books and uniforms. By empowering these students, we are lifting entire families out of the cycle of poverty.

Feeding the Starving

Annadanam (Food Donation)

"Annadanam Mahadanam" - the donation of food is considered the greatest of all charities. We ensure that no one in our vicinity goes to bed hungry.

Our volunteers prepare fresh, nutritious, and hygienic meals which are served freely to the destitute, wandering sadhus, and the starving. This sacred feeding is conducted every single week, and on a massive scale during all major Hindu festivals like Maha Shivaratri and Pradosham.

A Safe Haven

Ashram & Shelter

For those who have lost everything and find themselves on the streets, the Siva Sadhanam Ashram provides a warm, safe, and loving environment.

Our ashram is more than just a roof; it is a community of care. We provide homeless individuals with a bed, daily meals, medical assistance if required, and a peaceful environment where they can pray, recover, and live with dignity under the protective gaze of Lord Shiva.
