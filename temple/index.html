The Temple & Deity

Sit a while in the presence of Mahadeva. Find peace, clarity, and grace.

Lord Shiva

Shiva is Mahadeva, the great God: destroyer of ignorance, lord of yogis, and the gentlest giver to those who call on him with a sincere heart. He is worshipped in our temple as the Shiva Linga, the formless symbol of the infinite.

The crescent moon on his brow speaks of calm, the Ganga flowing from his hair represents divine grace, and the blue throat of Neelakantha reminds us of his compassion for the world.

ॐ नमः शिवाय

The five-syllable mantra Na-Ma-Shi-Va-Ya can be chanted anywhere, by anyone, at any hour to invoke his presence.

Visiting Guidelines

  • Darshan is open to all devotees regardless of background.
  • Please remove footwear before entering the temple premises.
  • Modest and traditional attire is requested.
  • Offerings like Bilva leaves, flowers, and fruits can be given to the priests.

Daily Puja Timings

Time Puja / Seva
5:30 AM Suprabhatam
The temple opens with morning hymns.
6:30 AM Ushakala Abhishekam
Sacred bathing of the Linga with milk and water.
8:00 AM Morning Archana
Chanting of names and offerings.
12:00 PM Uchikala Puja & Aarti
Midday worship. Temple closes post aarti.
Temple closed in the afternoon
5:00 PM Temple Reopens
Evening darshan begins.
6:00 PM Sayarakshai Deepa Aradhana
Lighting of evening lamps.
8:00 PM Arthajama Puja
Final worship, followed by Prasadam distribution.

Note: On Mondays (Somavaram) and major festival days, timings may be extended. Please contact us to confirm.
