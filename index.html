A Mission to Serve Humanity

Founded in 1999 by K Subramaniya Swami, Siva Sadhanam Trust is dedicated to the welfare of the poor, the starving, and the homeless through the grace of Lord Shiva.

Explore Our Sevas Read Our History

Our Three Pillars of Service

Every offering made to the trust goes directly towards uplifting those in need. We see service to humanity as the highest form of worship.

Education for the Poor

We believe education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty. The trust pays school and college fees for students who have the will to learn but lack the financial means.

Learn More

Annadanam (Food Donation)

No one should go to sleep hungry. We conduct massive Annadanam drives every week and on all major Hindu festivals, ensuring the starving are fed with dignity and respect.

Learn More

Ashram Shelter

Our ashram provides a safe, warm, and spiritually uplifting environment for homeless people. We offer them a place to stay, heal, and find peace in community living.

Learn More
