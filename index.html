Welcome, dear devotees

Siva Sadhanam Trust exists so that every family in our community has a place to sit quietly before Lord Shiva, to offer a lamp, and to feel held by something larger than the day’s worries.

Here the bell rings at dawn and the lamps are lit at dusk. Elders share stories, children learn the shlokas, and no one who comes hungry leaves without prasadam. Everything we do is made possible by devotees like you.

About Us

A community gathered around a simple wish: to keep Lord Shiva’s worship alive and to serve those around us.

Our story

Siva Sadhanam Trust began when a few devotees started meeting to chant, light lamps and share a meal. What was a small gathering grew into a temple and a trust, held together by volunteers and by the generosity of families who kept returning.

Today the trust runs daily worship, celebrates Shiva’s festivals with the whole community, serves annadana, and supports the education of children. We see worship and service as one practice.

Temple & Deity

Lord Shiva

Shiva is Mahadeva, the great God: destroyer of ignorance, lord of yogis, and the gentlest giver to those who call on him with a sincere heart. He is worshipped as the Shiva Linga, the formless symbol of the infinite, and as Nataraja, the Lord of the cosmic dance.

The crescent moon on his brow speaks of calm, the Ganga flowing from his hair of grace, and the blue throat of Neelakantha of his compassion, for he held the world’s poison so that others might live.

Visiting the temple

  • Darshan is open to all devotees.
  • Please remove footwear before the entrance and dress modestly.
  • Bilva leaves, flowers, milk and fruit may be offered at the abhishekam counter.
  • Prasadam is served after the evening aarti.
  • Elders and children are welcome to sit in the mandapam.

Festivals we celebrate

✦

Maha Shivaratri

The Great Night of Shiva. Devotees fast and keep an all-night vigil with four prahar pujas, abhishekam and bhajans.

Pradosham

Observed twice every lunar month on Trayodashi. Special abhishekam and worship of Nandi and Shiva at twilight.

Karthigai Deepam

The festival of lights, when rows of lamps are lit around the temple in honour of Shiva as endless flame.

Thiruvathirai

Arudra Darshan of Lord Nataraja in the month of Margazhi, with special abhishekam at dawn.

Annabhishekam

The Linga is adorned with cooked rice, then shared as prasadam with all devotees.

Karthika Masa Somavaram

Mondays of the sacred month of Karthika, with lamp offerings and special archana.

Festival dates follow the Hindu panchang and shift each year. We update this calendar as dates are confirmed.
