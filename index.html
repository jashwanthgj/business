Our Story

A Journey of Devotion & Service

Siva Sadhanam Trust began as a simple gathering of devotees united by a shared love for Lord Shiva. What started with humble prayers has blossomed into a thriving spiritual center in Jangalapalli Village, Tiruttani.

We believe that true worship extends beyond the sanctum. It is found in the smiles of the children we educate and the satisfaction of those we feed through our Annadana programs. We welcome everyone, from all walks of life, to find peace here.

Open to All

Come as you are. Leave lighter.

2010

Humble Beginnings

Devotees begin regular gatherings for Shiva puja and bhajans in the village.

2015

Establishment of the Trust

The Siva Sadhanam Trust is officially formed, and the sacred temple sanctum is established.

2018

Expanding our Seva

Formal Annadana (food donation) and education seva programs are launched to support the local community.

Today

A Growing Family

Daily pujas, grand festival celebrations, and an ever-growing family of devotees coming together.

Daily Schedule

Temple Puja Timings

Join us in our daily offerings to Mahadeva. The temple is open for darshan during these sacred hours.

5:30 AM

Suprabhatam

The temple opens with morning hymns.

6:30 AM

Ushakala Abhishekam

Sacred bathing of the Shiva Linga.

8:00 AM

Morning Archana

Chanting of names and offerings by devotees.

12:00 PM

Uchikala Puja

Midday worship. Temple closes post aarti.

5:00 PM

Temple Reopens

Evening darshan begins.

6:00 PM

Sayarakshai Deepa Aradhana

Lighting of lamps at dusk.

8:00 PM

Arthajama Puja

Final worship of the day followed by prasadam.

* Timings may vary slightly on Pradosham, Maha Shivaratri, and other festival days.
