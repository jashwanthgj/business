Events & Festivals

Come celebrate with the community. Everyone is welcome.

Weekly Regular Events

Somavara (Monday) Puja

Every Monday is dedicated to Lord Shiva. We hold special evening Abhishekam, Deepa Aradhana, and collective chanting of the Panchakshara Mantra.

Weekly Annadanam Drive

Our volunteers gather to cook and distribute hundreds of meals to the poor and destitute in and around Tiruttani. Want to volunteer? Join us.

Major Festivals Celebrated

Maha Shivaratri

The Great Night of Shiva. Devotees fast and keep an all-night vigil with four prahar pujas, continuous abhishekam, and bhajans. Massive Annadanam is organized the following morning.

Pradosham

Observed twice every lunar month on Trayodashi (13th day). We perform special abhishekam and twilight worship of Nandi and Shiva to absolve karma.

Karthigai Deepam

The festival of lights, honoring Lord Shiva as the endless flame of consciousness. The entire trust premises are illuminated with rows of oil lamps.

Annabhishekam

During the Tamil month of Aippasi, the Shiva Linga is grandly adorned entirely with cooked rice, which is later distributed as sacred prasadam to the hungry.

Thiruvathirai (Arudra Darshan)

Celebrated in Margazhi month, honoring the cosmic dance of Lord Nataraja. Special dawn abhishekam and Kali (sweet dish) offering are held.

Karthika Masa Somavaram

Mondays of the sacred month of Karthika are celebrated with grand lamp offerings (Deepa Seva), Rudrabhishekam, and mass chanting.
