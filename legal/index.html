Legal & Policies

Privacy, Terms of Use, and Donation Guidelines.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Privacy & Cookie Policy

Siva Sadhanam Trust ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy applies to information collected through our website (sivasadhanam.om.to).

Information We Collect

When you contact us, book a seva, or make a donation, we may collect personal information such as your Name, Phone Number (WhatsApp), Email Address, and Postal Address. If you make a donation, we collect transaction details to issue your receipt and maintain legal accounting records.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process your donations and issue official tax receipts.
  • To communicate with you regarding your queries, sevas, or trust updates.
  • To maintain internal records as required by Indian Trust and Tax laws.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Cookies Policy

Our website is a static informational site. However, we may use third-party services (such as Google Maps for directions or basic analytics to understand website traffic) which may place small data files called "cookies" on your device. By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of these standard cookies.

3. Donation & Refund Policy

Siva Sadhanam Trust is a registered non-profit organization. We rely on the generous donations of devotees to fund our Temple Maintenance, Annadanam, and Education Sevas.

General Donation Policy

By making a donation to Siva Sadhanam Trust, you acknowledge that the trust will use the funds at its discretion for the charitable purposes outlined on our website. Once a donation is made for a specific cause, we will make every effort to utilize it for that cause.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

As a charitable trust, we follow a strict No Refund policy for donations once they have been successfully processed.

However, we understand that accidental transactions can occur. A refund may be considered under the following exceptional circumstances:

  • If a transaction is duplicated due to a technical error (e.g., your account was debited twice for the same transaction).
  • If you accidentally entered an incorrect amount (e.g., ₹10,000 instead of ₹1,000).

How to request a refund: If you face such an error, you must contact us at sivasadhanam@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction. You must provide proof of the transaction (bank statement or UPI reference number). Refunds will be processed at the discretion of the Trust Board and, if approved, will be credited back to the original payment source within 10 to 15 working days.
